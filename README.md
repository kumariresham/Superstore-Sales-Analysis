# Sample Superstore – Sales & Profit Analysis

## 1. Project Overview
This project analyzes the Sample Superstore dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, regions, shipping methods, products, and discount patterns.

**Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Google Colab

**Dataset:** Sample - Superstore.csv  
**Total Records:** 9,994  
**Total Columns:** 21

## 2. Project Objectives
- Analyze total sales and profit.
- Identify profitable and loss-making products.
- Compare sales across regions and categories.
- Understand customer segments and shipping methods.
- Analyze the relationship between discounts and profitability.
- Present findings using graphs and summary tables.

## 3. Key Business Findings

### Overall Performance
- Total Sales: $2,297,200.86
- Total Profit: $286,397.02
- Overall Profit Margin: 12.47%

### Category and Regional Analysis
- Technology had the highest category profit margin at 17.40%.
- The West region had the highest regional sales at $725,457.82.
- New York City had the highest city sales at $256,368.16.

### Customer Segment Analysis
- Consumer had the highest total sales: $1,161,401.34.
- Home Office had the highest profit margin: 14.03%.

### Shipping Analysis
- Standard Class had the highest sales: $1,358,215.74.
- Same Day had the highest average sales per order: $486.22.

### Discount Analysis
- 0% Discount generated the highest sales: $1,087,908.47.
- 0% Discount generated the highest total profit: $320,987.60.
- 20% Discount generated the second-highest sales: $764,594.37.
- Discount groups from 30% to 80% recorded negative total profit.
- 10% Discount had the highest average profit per record: $96.06.
- 50% Discount had the lowest average profit per record: -$310.70.

## 4. Business Recommendations
1. Review high-discount transactions to understand why they are associated with losses.
2. Study profitable products and customer segments to identify successful sales patterns.
3. Monitor profit margins along with total sales when making pricing decisions.
4. Review loss-making products and consider pricing or cost improvements.
5. Continue tracking regional and shipping performance.

## 5. Conclusion
This project helped me practice data analysis using Python. I learned how to group data, calculate sales and profit, compare business performance, identify patterns, and create graphs.

The analysis shows that high sales do not always mean high profit. Profit margin and loss analysis are also important for business decisions.
